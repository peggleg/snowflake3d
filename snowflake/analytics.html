Analytics & cookies

The generator works whether you accept or reject optional analytics. No Google Analytics tag loads until you accept it. Use Cookie settings below to change your choice.

What we measure

With your permission, Google Analytics measures visits and counts of generated designs, downloads and copied design links. If enabled in the stream, scroll-depth events also measure whether the bottom of the workshop page is reached. Google also processes browser/device information, pseudonymous cookie identifiers and approximate location as part of providing analytics.

Our events exclude entered words, messages, seeds, design recipes, filenames and copied URLs. We send a fixed workshop page address, without its query string or fragment. The privacy-enquiry page is not tracked. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in our tag configuration.

What is stored on your device

The necessary preference sf3d_privacy_v1 cookie remembers only your analytics choice and its expiry for 180 days. If you accept analytics, first-party cookies whose names start with sf3d_ga store Google's analytics identifiers for up to 180 days. These identifiers are separate from your snowflake seed.

Rejecting or withdrawing consent stops our future analytics collection. Withdrawing removes the analytics cookies available to this page and reloads it without the tag. It does not erase data already sent to Google. Your browser can also remove cookies. If cookies are blocked, we may ask again when you revisit.

Your designs and enquiries

Generation happens in your browser, but opening a shared design URL sends its encoded recipe to hosting as part of the page request. There is no website design or enquiry database. Privacy-enquiry emails are kept while handled and normally deleted within 90 days after resolution, with limited exceptions for an ongoing complaint, dispute or legal obligation. Email providers may retain separate technical or backup records.

Make a privacy enquiry. For Google's processing, see How Google uses information. Hosting and form security are explained in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and its Turnstile Privacy Addendum.
